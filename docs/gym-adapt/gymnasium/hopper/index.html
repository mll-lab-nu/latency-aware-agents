02 · Frozen contract

Native 3D control and the complete 11D observation order

Runtime facts

  • Hopper-v4, Gymnasium 0.29.1, MuJoCo 3.3.7.
  • Action: Box(-1,1,(3,),float32).
  • Observation: Box(-inf,inf,(11,),float64).
  • dt=0.008 s, frame skip 4, model timestep 0.002 s.
  • RGB render: 480×480×3 through NVIDIA EGL.

Experiment decisions

  • Torque order: thigh, leg, foot; each clipped to [-1,1].
  • Pending no-op: [0,0,0].
  • State order: torso height/angle, three joint angles, torso x/z/angular velocities, and three joint angular velocities.
  • 125 Hz environment and observation clocks; one raw frame per decision.
  • Small-model training latency: zero raw frames.
RGB+state boundary: Sample Factory trains directly on the native 11D observation. Teacher export pairs the same ordered state with raw RGB, and QwenOFT consumes the native 3D action without bridge padding.

05 · Method and definitions

Returns and lengths are environment-native closed-loop outcomes

  • Each latency uses ten deterministic episode IDs and seed 3333.
  • Latency is measured in raw Hopper frames: L1 is 8 ms, L2 16 ms, L3 24 ms, and L4 32 ms.
  • The scheduler holds the current action and applies issued actions in FIFO order once ready.
  • Return is the sum of native healthy, forward, and control-cost rewards.
  • Length is raw environment steps before unhealthy termination or the 1,000-step TimeLimit.

07 · VLA training and failure diagnosis

Low offline loss does not survive closed-loop Hopper control

0.00788final train loss
0.01000final validation loss
173.136VLA L0 mean return
86.35VLA L0 mean length

QwenOFT completed 5,000 SFT steps and produced a numerically clean offline fit, but all 20 L0 evaluation episodes terminate after 82–93 environment steps. At L2, all 20 episodes terminate at step 10 with mean return 9.513. A same-seed small-model control reaches 2,971.510 mean return and 820.3 mean length, with 13 of 20 episodes reaching the 1,000-step limit.

What was ruled out

  • State/action dimensions, ordering, normalization, prompt, final checkpoint, and L0 timing match the frozen contract.
  • Replaying recorded actions reproduces rewards and positions exactly, ruling out logger or action-application mismatch.
  • The policy changes when the state changes; the 11D state interface is active rather than silently replaced by zeros.
  • Initial evaluation states are covered by training initial states, and the same evaluation seeds do not rescue the VLA.

Primary failure mechanism

  • VLA online states leave the teacher-data manifold: median normalized nearest-state distance is 0.168, versus 0.005 for successful teacher control.
  • Nearest-state action MAE rises from 0.049 in steps 0–9 to 0.138 after step 50; the foot dimension contributes 0.266 MAE late in the episode.
  • The VLA foot-action mean flips from the teacher's −0.120 to +0.335, while thigh saturation disappears.
  • A teacher prefix of 1, 3, 10, or 50 steps does not improve survival; after a 50-step teacher prefix, VLA takeover still fails at mean total length 85.5.
Conclusion: this is closed-loop covariate shift under one-step behavior cloning, not evidence that RGB+state input is invalid. The return-gated dataset contains successful trajectories but almost no recovery or pre-fall correction states. Aggregate validation loss is dominated by steady-gait rows and does not expose the phase-specific control errors that push the policy out of distribution.

Most direct corrective experiment

  1. Roll out the current VLA and label its visited states with the small-model teacher (DAgger-style aggregation), including pre-fall and recovery states.
  2. Oversample initialization, gait transitions, and recovery corrections instead of uniformly sampling long steady-gait episodes.
  3. Track phase-specific action error and short closed-loop survival during training; do not select a model from aggregate one-step loss alone.
  4. Retrain on the augmented data before increasing SFT duration. More epochs over the same success-only distribution are unlikely to fix the failure.

09 · Limitations and next step

The teacher is usable at L0; the current VLA is not

  • One teacher-training seed and one VLA-training run were evaluated; neither result establishes multi-seed robustness.
  • The small-model training statistic 3,206.405 is not an evaluation mean; its original closed-loop L0 mean is 2,682.095.
  • Small-model L1–L4 and VLA L2 measure immediate unhealthy termination, not a degraded stable gait.
  • The return gate excludes low-return failures but also removes the recovery distribution needed by an imperfect learned controller.
  • The diagnostic nearest-neighbor action error is an interpretable teacher-reference proxy, not a counterfactual ground-truth action for every off-manifold state.
