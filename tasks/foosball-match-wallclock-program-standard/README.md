## Task
Table football on a physical table: you drive the four motorised rods of one team (goalkeeper rod with 3 figures, 2-rod 2, 5-rod 5, 3-rod 3) by slide and angle targets that the motors follow along a speed- and acceleration-limited path, and see the table only through an overhead camera; one game to 5 goals under the ITSF rules (opening sequence, possession time limits per rod, no spinning); in the `match` format the other team is a scripted computer team, in the `duel` format another player; format `match` (seats A),
action level `low`, program play. Generated by `tools/generate.py` from
`sports/foosball/sport.toml`; do not edit by hand.

## Difficulty
Set at run time: the seed (`RO_SEED`; it draws the match's random conditions).

## Reference solution
`solution/solve.sh` runs the sport's reference player (`solution/builtin_players.py`) through the core's client
(`solution/ro_client.py`): camera images and `/spec` only.

## Verification
The `sim` service (the v0.2 core, `environment/sim/core/`) records the match (`episode.jsonl`) and a fresh process
replays it (state hash); the verifier (`tests/grade.py` = `core/grading.py`, no network) writes the common reward
fields (`success`, `valid_trial`, `deterministic`, `<seat>_won`, ...) and the sport's fields (`tests/sport_grade.py`).

## Details
| | |
|---|---|
| Rules | [ITSF (International Table Soccer Federation) Rules of Table Soccer, Standard Matchplay Rules](https://s3.tablesoccer.org/organization/ITSF/rules/Standard_Matchplay_Rules_2024_en.pdf) version Version 2.0 (December 2023) |
| id | rule | deviation |
| D1 | 3 | one game to 5 points instead of a match of several games (the points and games targets are set by the event) |
| D2 | 3, 7.1 | a safety cap of 240 s of play or 40 rallies (the rulebook has no time limit); then the player with more goals wins, at equal goals the scorer of the last goal, at 0:0 the player with the ball less time in its own half, and if that is equal the player who did not have the first kick-off (the last resort; no draws) |
| D3 | 4, 5.1 | the initial flip is the run-time parameter FIRST_PLAYER (who has the first kick-off); the referee places the ball at rest beside the central figure of the restart rod (its side and a few millimetres along the rod from the seed) and play starts when the reviews are in, instead of the ready protocol |
| D4 | 5.2, 5.3 | no delay-of-game rule; the restart rod's possession clock already runs during the opening sequence (it starts with the possession clock figure) |
| D5 | 6, 7.1.1.1, 7.1.2, 17, 18 | fouls are always given as the possession award (5-rod or goalie rod), without the non-offending team's option to continue, warnings, penalty shots or time-outs; the pause between points is the review |
| D6 | 10, 15.2-15.3 | the table is fixed and rigid (no jarring or reset); the rods have mechanical stops at +-2.8 rad, so a contact spin cannot occur (the referee still checks it) |
| D7 | 7.3 | a transparent cover 4 cm above the playfield keeps the ball on the table, so illegal aerials and ball off table do not occur in normal play (the ball-off-table rule is implemented) |
| D8 | 2 | the figures of the two teams do not collide with each other, only with the ball |
| D9 | 7.2, 11, 12 | the passing and wall rules and playing the ball dead are not judged (they need a referee's judgement of a stopped, pinned or intentional ball); the dead-ball restart for a ball at rest that no rod can reach is judged |
