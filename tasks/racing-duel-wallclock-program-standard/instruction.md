# Robot racing: program play (duel)

You play robot racing against an opponent model in the same world (seat `A` or `B`). Drive a simulated 1:10-scale racecar (rigid chassis, four rolling wheels, wheel-speed and steering servos with finite force) round a seeded walled circuit with fixed obstacles, from the cameras on your car; one-lap heats after the RoboRacer Head-to-Head rules, with crash calls (the at-fault car is placed 2 m behind and warned, the third warning disqualifies) and obstacle crashes; format `match`: against a scripted car, the first to win two heats wins; `duel`: against another player under the same rules; `race`: four cars, one heat per car with a rotating grid, points per place and a classification by points.
This task is generated from `sports/racing/sport.toml` (task `racing-duel-wallclock-program-standard`).

## Time
The world runs in real time at 1/S of real speed (S = `timing.slowdown` in `game spec`); your thinking costs game time. At the end of every unit the world freezes for the reviews.

Whenever the world freezes for a review (after every unit: a point, a shot, a heat; see `game spec`), send `game ready "WHY" "PLAN"` (a short review) before the next unit starts.
The match is graded when your session ends: stay until it is over. **If you never send `game start`, your side loses the match** (unless no side started), also when the sessions end before the start window closes.

## Write a program
You may write a program that observes, measures in the images, computes your next action and sends it, without a
new model response for each action. You stay in the loop: watch its log, and stop, fix and restart it whenever
needed. Only you send `game start` and `game ready`.

- Use only the camera images, the service's replies and this document; no simulator internals or hidden state.
- One action at a time: an action sent before the previous one has returned is refused (HTTP 429).
- Your container: Python 3.12 with numpy, SciPy and Pillow; no internet during the match. Keep your scripts, logs
  and notes in `/app/output/`.

## Interface
Use the `game` command, or the same calls as HTTP with JSON at `http://sim:8000/<SEAT>/` (your seat is `$SEAT`):

| `game` command | HTTP | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `game spec` | `GET /spec` | the actions (types, fields, ranges), the cameras with their intrinsics, the rule constants |
| `game status` | `GET /status` | the current state, no image |
| `game start` | `POST /start {}` | you are ready; the match begins when every seat has started |
| `game wait DT` | `POST /wait {"dt": DT}` | let DT game seconds pass (0 < DT <= 1) |
| `game observe` | `POST /observe {}` | the state and the camera images (one PNG of tiles: `observation.tiles`) |
| `game act JSON` | `POST /act JSON` | one action |
| `game drive SPEED STEERING` | `POST /act {"type":"drive", ...}` | one action (`game spec` lists the fields and ranges) |
| `game ready "WHY" "PLAN"` | `POST /ready {"why":"...","plan":"..."}` | your review after each of your actions |

Errors: 400 = an invalid action (nothing happens), 409 = not now (not your turn, or a review is due), 429 = one
action in flight, or more than 20 requests per second or 4 open requests from your seat (empty body: retry more slowly).

## Rules
- drive a simulated 1:10-scale racecar (rigid chassis, four rolling wheels, wheel-speed and steering servos with finite force) round a seeded walled circuit with fixed obstacles, from the cameras on your car; one-lap heats after the RoboRacer Head-to-Head rules, with crash calls (the at-fault car is placed 2 m behind and warned, the third warning disqualifies) and obstacle crashes; format `match`: against a scripted car, the first to win two heats wins; `duel`: against another player under the same rules; `race`: four cars, one heat per car with a rotating grid, points per place and a classification by points.
- `real_time`: this sport is real time (where the generic text above says otherwise, this line holds): the world runs while you think; `game wait DT` (POST /wait {"dt": DT}, 0 < DT <= 1) lets it run DT game seconds; `game ready "WHY" "PLAN"` is due only in the review after each heat, not after each action; acts are never out of turn; a command holds until replaced and takes effect at the next 10 ms control step; one act in flight per seat (429); acting while the world is frozen is refused (409)
- `action`: drive: {"type": "drive", "speed": S, "steering": D} (also `game drive S D`; a body without type is a drive): the target forward speed of the rear wheels S in [-1.5, 4.0] m/s (negative: reverse) and the front-wheel steering angle D in [-0.48, 0.48] rad (positive turns left); {"type": "none"} brakes and straightens the wheels; wheel motors and steering servos track the targets with finite force; numbers are rounded to 6 digits
- `camera`: two cameras ride on your car: `front` 0.22 m ahead of the car's centre and about 0.40 m above the road, pitched 20 degrees down; `chase` 1.5 m behind and about 1.5 m above, pitched about 35 degrees down; both 70 degrees vertical field of view, 240 x 160 pixels. `game observe` shows the front camera; `game observe '{"camera": "chase"}'` (POST /observe {"query": {"camera": "chase"}}) the chase camera. /spec `camera` gives the mounts, /spec `cameras` the front camera's world pose when /spec was read (it moves with the car). An image shows the world at the reply's `step`
- `track`: SI units, x/y on the flat ground, z up; a closed circuit of green asphalt with a yellow centre stripe, a white start/finish line and red/white walls on both sides, driven counter-clockwise: straights, a hairpin, an S-bend, a chicane and bends of both directions, 2.45-2.95 m wide, centre-line turn radius at least 2.5 m; 16 checkpoint lines across the full width, the 16th is the finish line, passed in order (reverse driving or cutting earns nothing); 2-4 fixed magenta obstacles (barrels and blocks), each leaving a free gap of at least 1.5 m on one side. The map is in /spec `geometry`: the centre line every about 0.5 m with the local width from the start/finish line in driving order, the lap length, the obstacles (position, yaw, size)
- `car`: identical cars: 0.60 m long, 0.45 m wide over the wheels, 0.46 m wheelbase, 0.10 m wheels, rear-wheel drive, front-wheel steering (the car turns down to a 0.9 m radius); friction, finite motor force and contacts decide the motion; walls, obstacles and cars collide physically
- `crashes`: touching is not penalized. A crash is a car-to-car contact that could change a car's speed by 0.30 m/s or more, or that lasts 2 s: the car that drove into the other (the larger speed towards the other car; if equal, the car behind) is at fault, is placed 2 m behind the other car at rest and gets a warning; the other car goes on; the third warning disqualifies. An obstacle contact that passes the same test is an obstacle crash: the car is placed at rest beside that obstacle, in its free gap, and held 1 s; no warning. Wall contact is not penalized, but you have to free your car yourself
- `match_format`: match: seat A against a scripted car (side `opponent`, race.py car B) of level RCB_LEVEL 1-4 that cruises its line at 1.75 / 2.2 / 2.7 / 3.2 m/s, steers round the obstacles, passes a car ahead in its line and keeps its distance; up to three one-lap heats, the first to win two heats wins; heat 1: FIRST_PLAYER (A by default) starts on the outer side, heat 2 the sides switch, heat 3 the seed's draw decides who starts inside; a heat is won by the first car across the finish line after all 16 checkpoints; success = winning the race
- `duel_format`: duel: seats A and B, the same rules as the match
- `race_format`: race: four cars (seats A-D), four one-lap heats; staggered grid behind the line (slot 1, pole, inner side; FIRST_PLAYER on pole in heat 1), rotated by one slot per heat; a heat ends when all running cars but one have finished, 10 game s after the first finisher, or at the time limit; points per heat place 25, 18, 15, 12; classification by points, then more first places, then more second places, ..., then a draw fixed by the seed; a car that has not started when the start window closes, misses a review or is disqualified is retired (parked off the track, no more points, classified after the running cars); a valid race always has a full classification (<seat>_place)
- `observation`: the state in every reply holds the heat and its game time (`heat_time_s`), your car (`own`: forward speed, command, grid slot and side inner or outer, held after an obstacle crash, retired), the race score (`race`: heats won or points, warnings, retired cars), the referee's crash calls of this heat (every car-to-car crash, your own obstacle crashes) and `results`: one entry per finished heat (winner, order, how it was decided, each car's checkpoints, finish time and obstacle crashes); no position, track progress or running order of any car during a heat, no depth or LiDAR, and no seed
- `review`: after every heat the world freezes; each running car's player sends `game ready "WHY" "PLAN"` within the review limit (else: duel, it loses the race; race, its car is retired); the next heat starts when every running car has reviewed
- Winner: match and duel: a car that gets its third warning loses the race; otherwise the first car to win two heats wins (a heat goes to the first car across the finish line; with no finisher to more progress, then to the car that reached it first, then, as the last resort not from the rulebook, to a draw fixed by the seed). Race: the classification by points, then count-back (more first places, then more second places, ...), then, as the last resort not from the rulebook, a draw fixed by the seed. There are no draws.
- Deviation D1 (rulebook Head-to-Head Race > Evaluation [2.5.4.1]): one lap per heat (official: the first car to complete 20 laps wins)
- Deviation D2 (rulebook Head-to-Head Race > Single Elimination, up to three heats [2.5.1.8]; Time Trial > Evaluation (random method)): first to two heat wins as written; the race slot is replaced by a heat time limit of the lap length at 1.25 m/s rounded up to 5 s (55-60 game s on the official circuits); with no finisher the rulebook's completed laps and progress decide (checkpoint-bounded distance, millimetres), then who reached that progress first, then, as the stated last resort, a draw fixed by the seed; two finishers in one 10 ms step are ordered by the interpolated crossing time
- Deviation D3 (rulebook Head-to-Head Race, starting positions [2.5.1.9]): there is no Time Trial: in heat 1 FIRST_PLAYER starts on the outer side, in heat 2 the sides switch (as written), in heat 3 a draw fixed by the seed (the rulebook's random method) gives the inner side (the legacy task gave it to the faster winning lap of heats 1-2; between cars of equal pace that was an artefact of the control timing and always the car that started inside in heat 1); the cars are 0.84 m apart centre to centre (official: about 30 cm)
- Deviation D4 (rulebook Race penalties, crash into the opponent [2.5.3.4, 2.5.1.14]): decided by the simulator, not by referees: a car-car contact is a crash when its impulse could change a car's speed by at least 0.30 m/s or the cars stay in contact for 2 s (touching below that is not penalized, as written); at fault is the car that drove into the other (closing speed larger by more than 0.1 m/s), else the car behind on the track, else the seed's draw; with more than two cars every pair is judged on its own; the race is not paused: only the at-fault car is placed 2 m behind the other car, at rest (further back in 0.5 m steps if that spot is taken), the other car is not stopped
- Deviation D5 (rulebook Race violations, warnings [2.5.1.14.6-8]): intent cannot be judged: every at-fault car-car crash is a warning; the third warning disqualifies the car from the race (head-to-head: the other car wins it; race: the car is retired, parked off the track and classified after the running cars); deliberate crowding and abnormal changes of direction are not judged beyond the crash rule
- Deviation D6 (rulebook Race penalties, crash into an obstacle [2.5.3.3]): decided by the simulator with the car-car test: the car is placed at rest at the side of the track 0.75 m before the obstacle, in its free gap, and held for 1 s; no warning; obstacles are fixed bodies (nothing to put back)
- Deviation D7 (rulebook Race penalties, crash into the track border [2.5.3.3]): the border is a continuous rigid wall: wall contact is not penalized, the car has to free itself (reverse); the time lost is the penalty; no manual handling
- Deviation D10 (rulebook Multi-car race (not in the RoboRacer rules; format and classification after the FIA 2026 Formula 1 Sporting Regulations)): format race: 4 cars (race.py supports 3-6), one one-lap heat per car; staggered grid of two columns 0.9 m apart, rotated by one slot per heat so every car starts once from every slot; a heat ends when all running cars but one have finished, 10 game s after the first finisher, or at the time limit; its order: finishers by finish time, then progress, then who reached it first, then the seed's draw; points 25-18-15-12-10-8 per place; classification by points, then count-back, then a draw fixed by the seed (no ties; the legacy task used the better place in the last heat, which with cars of equal pace always went to the car on pole in the last heat, a slot fixed by FIRST_PLAYER); a car that does not start in the start window, misses a review or is disqualified is retired (parked off the track, no more points) and classified after the running cars, the later retirement first

## Geometry and physics
Everything you need is in `game spec`: the world frame, the release point, the cameras (pose, field of view, size,
`focal_px`, `principal_point_px`; pinhole: u = cx + f * x_right / depth, v = cy - f * y_up / depth) and the
execution scatter. The physics engine is mujoco (CPU, 500 Hz rigid-body cars with wheel contacts and servo-driven wheels and steering, rigid walls and fixed obstacles).
